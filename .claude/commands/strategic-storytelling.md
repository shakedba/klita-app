# Strategic Narrative

## Overview
This skill applies Andy Raskin's strategic narrative structure and Nancy Duarte's presentation frameworks to craft compelling product stories, pitches, and presentations.

## Five-Act Structure

1. **Old World** - Describes how things functioned previously
2. **Insight** - Reveals what shifted and why timing matters now
3. **New World** - Illustrates newly available possibilities
4. **Stakes** - Establishes consequences of action or inaction
5. **Your Role** - Positions your product as the winning solution

## Implementation Checklist

**Structural Elements:**
- Establish a relatable problem in the opening
- Explain the catalyst for change
- Paint the desired future state
- Create urgency through meaningful consequences
- Present your solution clearly

**Delivery Principles:**
- Position your audience as the protagonist
- Balance emotional resonance with logical arguments
- Ground ideas in concrete examples
- Include a definitive call to action

## Key Principles

Raskin emphasizes that "The best product stories make the customer the hero, not your product."

Duarte advises that "The audience doesn't need to tune themselves to you—you need to tune your message to them."

These frameworks work together to create narratives that resonate, motivate, and drive action.
