# Culture Building Framework

This skill guides team culture design using frameworks from industry leaders David Singleton (Stripe) and Dharmesh Shah (HubSpot).

## Core Activation Points
The framework activates when addressing team building, value definition, ritual creation, or excellence standards.

## Key Frameworks

**Stripe's Operating Principles** emphasize:
- Moving swiftly while maintaining long-term vision
- Embracing transparency
- Taking ownership across domains

**Culture as Competitive Advantage** follows a four-step sequence:
1. Articulate what excellence looks like
2. Recruit for cultural contribution (not conformity)
3. Establish reinforcing practices
4. Make values tangible throughout the organization

## Implementation Structure

The template guides teams through:
- Identifying 3-5 core values with concrete interpretations
- Specifying what excellence entails behaviorally
- Establishing weekly, monthly, and quarterly practices that embed values
- Aligning hiring toward cultural values

## Notable Insights

Singleton: "Culture is what happens when no one is watching."

Shah: "Culture is to recruiting as product is to marketing."

Authentic culture emerges through everyday choices rather than formal declarations, and cultural strength directly impacts talent acquisition effectiveness.
