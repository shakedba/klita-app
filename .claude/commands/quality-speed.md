# Quality vs Speed Framework

This skill helps you navigate the tension between shipping quickly and maintaining excellence, drawing from Dylan Field's (Figma) philosophy on craft and Brian Chesky's (Airbnb) attention to meaningful details.

## Core Decision Matrix

**Invest in High Craft When:**
- The feature is core to your product
- Users interact with it frequently
- It creates competitive advantage
- It's a brand-defining moment

**Move Fast When:**
- Building internal tools
- Running experiments
- Creating non-core features
- Working behind-the-scenes

## The Key Insight

Field emphasizes that "AI makes design, craft, and quality the new moat for startups"—meaning excellence in the right areas becomes your defensible advantage. Chesky's perspective that "Leaders are in the details, but only the details that matter" clarifies the critical distinction: not all polish creates value.

## Quick Assessment

Use this decision template by evaluating whether your feature is:
- User-facing
- Part of the core product loop
- Frequently used
- Competitively important

These factors determine your craft investment level (high, medium, or low).

The framework prevents both extremes: shipping sloppy core experiences or over-polishing throwaway experiments.
