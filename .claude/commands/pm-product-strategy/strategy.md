# /strategy — Product Strategy Canvas

Build a comprehensive 9-section product strategy canvas. Works for ideas through mature products.

## Invocation

```
/strategy [describe your product]
/strategy [upload a PRD, pitch deck, or strategy doc]
/strategy                   # asks you about your product
```

## Workflow

### Step 1: Understand Your Product

Ask:
- What does the product do? What stage is it at?
- What's the business model?
- Who are the primary users and customers?
- What's the competitive landscape?

### Step 2: Build the Strategy Canvas

Work through nine strategic areas:

1. **Vision** — Inspiring north star; why does this product exist?
2. **Target Segments** — Who you serve, framed as Jobs to Be Done
3. **Pain Points & Value** — Problems solved for each segment
4. **Value Propositions** — Benefit statements in before/how/after format
5. **Strategic Trade-offs** — What you explicitly won't do (creates focus)
6. **Key Metrics** — North Star metric and quarterly priorities
7. **Growth Engine** — Customer acquisition strategy and unit economics
8. **Core Capabilities** — Required resources and build/partner/buy decisions
9. **Defensibility** — Competitive moat and barriers to imitation

Apply **product-strategy** skill.

### Step 3: Generate Documentation

```
## Product Strategy Canvas: [Product]

**Date**: [today]

### Vision
[Inspiring, one-sentence north star]

### Target Segments
| Segment | Primary JTBD | Size |
|---------|-------------|------|

### Pain Points & Value
[Problems solved for each segment]

### Value Propositions
[Before/How/After for each segment]

### Strategic Trade-offs
We will NOT: [explicit exclusions that create focus]

### Key Metrics
- North Star: [metric]
- Quarterly priorities: [metrics]

### Growth Engine
[Acquisition strategy and unit economics]

### Core Capabilities
[Required competencies and build/partner decisions]

### Defensibility
[Competitive moat and barriers to entry]
```

Save as markdown.

### Step 4: Offer Next Steps

- "Want me to **run a market scan** (SWOT + PESTLE + Porter's Five Forces + Ansoff)?"
- "Should I **design a pricing strategy** based on this?"
- "Want me to **define OKRs** for the next quarter?"
- "Should I **create a Lean Canvas** or **Startup Canvas**?"

## Key Principle

"A good strategy is more about what you say NO to than what you say YES to" — trade-offs are central, not secondary.
