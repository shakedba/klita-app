# /business-model — Business Model Exploration

Explore and document your business model using four complementary frameworks.

## Invocation

```
/business-model [describe your product]
/business-model lean [product]
/business-model startup [product]
/business-model all [product]
/business-model [upload pitch deck, PRD, or business plan]
```

## Four Framework Modes

### 1. Lean Canvas
Best for: early-stage ideas, startups, new product lines

Focuses on problems, solutions, metrics, and riskiest assumptions. Fast but introduces redundancy between sections.

Apply **lean-canvas** skill.

### 2. Full Business Model Canvas
Best for: established products, corporate strategy, investor materials

Maps nine interconnected building blocks across value creation, delivery, and financial viability.

Apply **business-model** skill.

### 3. Startup Canvas *(Recommended for new products)*
Best for: new products needing both strategic clarity AND a business model

Combines product strategy (9 sections) with business model details in one artifact. Separates strategy from business model — unlike Lean Canvas and BMC which mix them.

Apply **startup-canvas** skill.

### 4. Value Proposition Mode
Best for: refining messaging, product-market fit analysis, sales enablement

Uses a 6-part JTBD framework to articulate customer value.

Apply **value-proposition** skill.

## Workflow

1. Select mode — or run all four with `/business-model all`
2. Gather context — product description, stage, audience, constraints
3. Generate framework — structured markdown output
4. Offer follow-ups — stress-test assumptions, pricing strategy, deeper analysis

## Key Guidance

**Startup Canvas is the recommended starting point for new products** — it separates strategy from business model, preventing muddy thinking.

For `/business-model all`, run all four frameworks and compare: "Where do they agree? What tensions exist?"
