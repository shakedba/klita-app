# Strategic Prototyping: Build What Matters

This skill helps distinguish high-impact work from busy-work using frameworks from product leaders Shreyas Doshi and Marty Cagan.

## Core Frameworks

### 1. The LNO Framework
Categorizes work into three types:

**Leverage (10x multipliers)** - compounds over time and enables future work
- Design systems, core infrastructure, strategic features
- Should comprise ~70% of your work

**Neutral (1x necessary)** - maintains current state without compounding
- Bug fixes, minor UX tweaks, maintenance (~20% of work)

**Overhead (0x drain)** - busy-work that doesn't move the needle
- Excessive documentation, meetings about meetings (~10% maximum)

**Key principle:** Build when something will be reused 10+ times or unblocks future work. Otherwise, avoid premature abstraction.

### 2. Three Levels of Product Work

**Level 1 (Impact/Why)** - What outcome matters? Why does this move the business?

**Level 2 (Execution/How)** - Technical decisions and quality standards

**Level 3 (Optics/Perception)** - How stakeholders perceive the work

**Critical insight:** Teams often confuse execution for strategy. Always validate Level 1 before optimizing Level 2.

### 3. Pre-Mortems
Imagine failure six months ahead and work backward to identify risks before they happen. This surfaces blind spots and prevents costly mistakes.

### 4. Empowered vs Feature Teams
- **Feature teams** get features to build; success = shipping on time
- **Empowered teams** solve problems; success = business outcomes

Shift from "ship X by Q2" to "increase retention by 20%"

## Quick Checklist

Before committing to work:
- [ ] Classified as Leverage, Neutral, or Overhead
- [ ] Level 1 impact crystal clear with success metric
- [ ] Pre-mortem completed
- [ ] Top risks mitigated
- [ ] Solving a problem, not just building a feature
