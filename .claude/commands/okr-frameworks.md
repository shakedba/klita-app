# OKR & Goal Setting Framework

This skill teaches effective OKR writing using Christina Wodtke's methodology and Google's approach. It activates when setting quarterly goals, aligning teams, or tracking progress.

## Core Structure

OKRs pair inspiring qualitative objectives with 3 measurable key results each. A strong objective motivates teams while key results define specific, quantified outcomes with clear targets.

## Quality Standards

**Strong objectives** are:
- Inspiring and qualitative
- Time-bound
- Ambitious

**Strong key results** are:
- Measurable with specific numbers
- Outcome-focused (not activity-based)
- Achievable at 60-70% confidence
- Clear on success criteria

## Execution Approach

Teams should complete weekly check-ins tracking:
- Progress percentage
- Blockers
- Needed support

The framework emphasizes this is an alignment tool, not a project management system.

## Key Insight

As Google notes, "If you're hitting 100% of your OKRs, you're not being ambitious enough." This reflects the expectation that well-calibrated goals should stretch teams toward meaningful achievements while maintaining realistic confidence levels.
