# Positioning Mastery Framework

This resource outlines **April Dunford's positioning methodology** for clarifying how products fit in markets and compete.

## Core Framework (5 Components)

1. **Target Customer** — Define who benefits most, not a broad audience
2. **Category** — Establish how customers mentally classify it
3. **Alternatives** — Identify what customers currently use instead
4. **Unique Value** — Articulate meaningful differences
5. **Proof** — Provide evidence through metrics or case studies

## Key Principle

Dunford emphasizes: "Positioning is the first thing you need to get right. Everything else flows from it." This framework prevents the common mistake of claiming "no competitors," which signals weak market demand to potential customers.

## Positioning Canvas Template

Answer each component systematically, then conclude with a one-sentence positioning statement:

For [target customer] who [problem], [product name] is a [category] that [unique value]. Unlike [alternatives], we [key differentiator]. Proven by [proof].

## When to Apply

- Product launches
- Value proposition refinement
- Market category selection
- Competitive differentiation strategies
