# PESTLE Analysis

A strategic tool that evaluates macro-environmental factors across six dimensions to identify opportunities and threats in the external environment.

## The Six Dimensions

**Political**
- Government policies, regulations, political stability
- Trade agreements and tariffs
- Licensing and operating requirements
- Political risk in target markets

**Economic**
- Economic growth and GDP trends
- Interest rates and inflation
- Currency fluctuations
- Consumer spending power and financing accessibility

**Social**
- Population demographics and trends
- Cultural shifts and attitudes
- Consumer behavior and digital adoption patterns
- Workforce trends and talent availability

**Technological**
- Emerging technologies (AI, blockchain, cloud, etc.)
- Digital transformation relevance
- R&D activity and innovation pace
- Automation and disruption potential

**Legal**
- Data protection and privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA)
- Employment law
- Intellectual property
- Industry-specific regulations

**Environmental**
- Carbon emissions and sustainability requirements
- ESG expectations from investors and customers
- Climate risk exposure
- Resource availability

## Output Format

```
## PESTLE Analysis: [Market/Product]

| Factor | Dimension | Current State | Impact (H/M/L) | Trend | Timeframe | Strategic Response |
|--------|-----------|--------------|---------------|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| [factor] | Political | [state] | [H/M/L] | ↑↓→ | [timeline] | [response] |

### Key Opportunities
1. [Factor creating opportunity]

### Key Threats
1. [Factor creating threat]

### Strategic Implications
[How these factors shape product/market strategy]
```

## Process

1. Identify 3–5 factors per category
2. Assess impact (High/Medium/Low) and probability
3. Determine trend direction (↑ increasing, ↓ decreasing, → stable)
4. Develop strategic responses: exploit opportunities, mitigate or comply with threats
