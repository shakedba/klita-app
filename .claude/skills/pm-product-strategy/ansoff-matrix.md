# Ansoff Matrix

A strategic planning tool that evaluates growth opportunities across a 2×2 grid combining products (existing/new) and markets (existing/new).

## The Four Quadrants

**Market Penetration** (Current Product + Current Market)
- Lowest risk
- Increase usage or sales in existing market
- Strategies: improved retention, deeper acquisition in familiar segments
- Timeline: 6–12 months
- Risk: Low

**Market Development** (Current Product + New Market)
- Medium risk
- Sell existing product to new customer segments or geographies
- Product is proven; market dynamics are unfamiliar
- Timeline: 12–18 months
- Risk: Medium

**Product Development** (New Product + Current Market)
- Medium risk
- Introduce new products or features to existing customer base
- Leverages established relationships
- Timeline: 12–24 months
- Risk: Medium

**Diversification** (New Product + New Market)
- Highest risk
- Entering entirely new domains with unproven offerings
- Timeline: 24+ months
- Risk: High

## Output Format

```
## Ansoff Matrix: [Product/Company]

| Strategy | Opportunity | Risk | Investment | Priority |
|----------|------------|------|-----------|----------|
| Market Penetration | [specifics] | Low | [est.] | [H/M/L] |
| Market Development | [specifics] | Medium | [est.] | [H/M/L] |
| Product Development | [specifics] | Medium | [est.] | [H/M/L] |
| Diversification | [specifics] | High | [est.] | [H/M/L] |

### Recommended Focus
[Which quadrant to prioritize and why]

### Sequencing
[Order of pursuit with rationale]
```

## Key Insight

Most companies should excel in one quadrant before expanding. Establish market penetration before progressing to riskier growth vectors.
