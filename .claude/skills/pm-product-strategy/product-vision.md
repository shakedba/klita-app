# Product Vision

Craft an inspiring, achievable vision statement that motivates teams and guides strategic decisions.

## What Makes a Great Vision

- **Inspiring**: Motivates teams toward committed action
- **Achievable**: Grounded in realistic market and resource constraints
- **Memorable**: Can be communicated in one sentence
- **Emotional**: Creates meaningful human connection
- **Directional**: Guides trade-off decisions

## Development Process

### Step 1: Gather Context
- Company mission and values
- Product stage and market position
- Target customers and their jobs to be done
- 3–5 year ambition

### Step 2: Define Core Elements
- **The problem**: What fundamental human or business problem does this solve?
- **The future state**: What does the world look like when you succeed?
- **The why**: Why does this matter beyond revenue?

### Step 3: Generate Vision Candidates

Create 3–5 vision statement variations across different registers:
- **Aspirational**: Big picture transformation
- **Customer-centric**: Focused on customer outcome
- **Market**: Defines the category you're creating
- **Values-led**: Grounded in principles

### Step 4: Evaluate and Select

Score each candidate:
| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|-------|
| Inspiring to team | 30% |
| Believable/achievable | 25% |
| Customer-centric | 25% |
| Memorable (1 sentence) | 20% |

### Step 5: Connect to Strategy

Show how the vision connects to:
- Company values
- Market opportunity
- Product strategy (use with **product-strategy** skill)

## Output Format

```
## Product Vision: [Product]

### Recommended Vision Statement
"[One sentence, inspiring and achievable]"

### Why This Works
[Rationale: what it signals, who it motivates, what it guides]

### Alternative Versions
1. [Aspirational version]
2. [Customer-centric version]
3. [Market-defining version]

### Vision → Strategy Connection
[How this vision shapes strategic trade-offs and priorities]
```

## Key Principle

"A great vision is memorable and can be communicated in one sentence" while maintaining both ambition and credibility. Vision is a living document — it evolves alongside strategy.
