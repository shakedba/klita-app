# SWOT Analysis

A strategic framework for evaluating a product or business across four dimensions: internal positives, internal negatives, external positives, and external threats.

## The Four Dimensions

**Strengths** (Internal, Positive)
- Unique capabilities and competitive advantages
- Strong brand recognition or customer loyalty
- Proprietary technology or data
- Cost advantages or economies of scale

**Weaknesses** (Internal, Negative)
- Resource or capability gaps
- Technical debt or infrastructure limitations
- Brand perception issues
- Process inefficiencies

**Opportunities** (External, Positive)
- Growing market segments
- Regulatory changes creating openings
- Technology shifts enabling new approaches
- Competitor weaknesses to exploit

**Threats** (External, Negative)
- Emerging competitors
- Regulatory changes creating risk
- Market contraction or commoditization
- Technology disruption of your model

## Process

1. Identify 5–7 items in each category
2. Cross-reference insights:
   - S + O: How can strengths capture opportunities?
   - W + T: How to fortify weaknesses against threats?
   - S + T: How can strengths mitigate threats?
   - W + O: What opportunities help overcome weaknesses?
3. Develop strategic recommendations

## Output Format

```
## SWOT Analysis: [Product/Company]

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| [strength 1] | [weakness 1] |
| [strength 2] | [weakness 2] |
| ... | ... |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---------------|--------|
| [opportunity 1] | [threat 1] |
| [opportunity 2] | [threat 2] |
| ... | ... |

### Strategic Actions

| Strategy | Based On | Action |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Build | S + O | [leverage strength to capture opportunity] |
| Defend | W + T | [address weakness to mitigate threat] |
| Pivot | W + O | [use opportunity to overcome weakness] |
| Protect | S + T | [use strength to defend against threat] |

### Top 3 Recommendations
1. [Recommendation]
2. [Recommendation]
3. [Recommendation]
```

## Key Guidance

- Context matters: compare against competitors and industry standards
- Update quarterly to keep analysis current with market conditions
- Use alongside PESTLE for macro context and Porter's Five Forces for industry dynamics
