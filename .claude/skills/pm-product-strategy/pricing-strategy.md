# Pricing Strategy

Design pricing strategies grounded in value delivery, competitive positioning, and customer willingness to pay.

## Pricing Models

| Model | Best For | Example |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Flat-rate | Simple, predictable | Basecamp $99/mo |
| Per-seat | Team collaboration | Slack |
| Usage-based | Infrastructure, APIs | AWS |
| Tiered (Free/Pro/Enterprise) | Broad market segmentation | Most SaaS |
| Freemium | Network effects products | Dropbox |
| Freemium + usage | Platform products | Twilio |
| Value-based | High-impact enterprise | Consulting tools |

## Framework

### 1. Understand Core Value
- What quantifiable outcomes does the product deliver?
- Time saved, revenue generated, risk reduced?
- What is the customer's alternative cost?

### 2. Choose Value Metric
What you charge for: seats, usage, outcomes, features, storage

### 3. Design Tier Structure
2–4 tiers with clear feature differentiation:
- **Free/Starter**: Acquisition tier, basic value
- **Pro**: Primary revenue driver, full value
- **Enterprise**: Custom contracts, advanced security/compliance

### 4. Competitive Analysis
- Map 3–5 competitor tier structures and price points
- Identify category pricing patterns
- Find gaps and differentiation opportunities

### 5. Willingness-to-Pay Assessment
- Van Westendorp price sensitivity surveys
- Value-based anchoring
- Founder-led customer conversations

### 6. Validate Assumptions
- A/B test pricing pages and anchors
- Pilot pricing with early customers
- Monitor conversion rates and churn by tier
