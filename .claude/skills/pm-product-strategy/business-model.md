# Business Model Canvas

A strategic tool for mapping how businesses create, deliver, and capture value through nine interconnected components.

## The Nine Building Blocks

### Value Creation (Left Side)
- **Key Partners**: Who do you work with? What do you source externally?
- **Key Activities**: What must you do well to deliver your value proposition?
- **Key Resources**: What assets are required?

### Value Proposition (Center)
- **Value Proposition**: What value do you deliver? What problem do you solve?

### Value Delivery (Right Side)
- **Customer Segments**: Who are your most important customers?
- **Channels**: How do you reach and deliver to customers?
- **Customer Relationships**: What type of relationship does each segment expect?

### Financial Viability (Bottom)
- **Cost Structure**: What are the most important costs?
- **Revenue Streams**: How does the business earn from each segment?

## Limitations

BMC lacks sections for vision, competitive defensibility, strategic trade-offs, and key performance metrics. Use **startup-canvas** for new products needing both strategic and business model clarity.

## Best For

Established businesses, corporate strategy, investor materials requiring operational clarity.
