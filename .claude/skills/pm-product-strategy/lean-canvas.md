# Lean Canvas

A startup-focused adaptation of the Business Model Canvas that replaces Partners/Activities/Resources with Problem/Solution/Unfair Advantage. Designed for rapid hypothesis testing.

## The Nine Components

### Product Definition
- **Problem**: Top 3 problems your customers face
- **Solution**: Top 3 features that address those problems
- **Unique Value Proposition**: Single, clear message that states why you're different and worth buying
- **Unfair Advantage**: Something that cannot easily be copied or bought

### Market & Traction
- **Customer Segments**: Target customers and early adopters
- **Channels**: Path to reach customers
- **Revenue Streams**: Revenue model, pricing, lifetime value

### Economics & Validation
- **Cost Structure**: Customer acquisition costs, distribution costs, hosting, people
- **Key Metrics**: Key activities that drive your business

## Limitations

- Known redundancy: problem overlaps with customer segments; solution overlaps with UVP
- Lacks vision, strategic trade-offs, and coherence validation
- Use **startup-canvas** when you need both strategic clarity AND a business model

## Best For

Brainstorming and early-stage ideation when speed matters over completeness.
