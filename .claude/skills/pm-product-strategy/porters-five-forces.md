# Porter's Five Forces

A strategic tool for evaluating industry attractiveness by examining five competitive dynamics. Reveals which forces most threaten profitability and informs competitive positioning.

## The Five Forces

### 1. Competitive Rivalry
How intense is competition among existing players?
- Number and size of competitors
- Industry growth rate
- Product differentiation
- Exit barriers
- Price competition intensity

### 2. Supplier Power
How much leverage do suppliers have?
- Number of suppliers
- Uniqueness of inputs
- Switching costs
- Supplier concentration

### 3. Buyer Power
How much leverage do customers have?
- Customer concentration
- Purchase volume
- Switching costs
- Price sensitivity
- Availability of substitutes

### 4. Threat of Substitutes
How easily can customers switch to alternatives?
- Availability of substitute products
- Price-performance of substitutes
- Customer willingness to switch
- Emerging technologies enabling new substitutes

### 5. Threat of New Entrants
How easy is it for new competitors to enter?
- Capital requirements
- Regulatory barriers
- Economies of scale
- Brand loyalty and switching costs
- Access to distribution

## Key Insight

The same industry can be attractive for some companies and unattractive for others, depending on strategic positioning and competitive advantages. Focus on the specific forces most threatening your profitability.
