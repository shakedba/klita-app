# Stakeholder Orchestration

This skill applies Radical Candor and interpersonal dynamics to manage relationships and navigate feedback conversations effectively.

## Core Principle

The framework balances two essential elements:
- **Personal care** for the individual
- **Direct challenge** to the issue

This combination avoids three pitfalls:
- Sacrificing honesty for kindness (ruinous empathy)
- Delivering criticism without compassion (obnoxious aggression)
- Masking feedback entirely (manipulative insincerity)

## Practical Application

The **SBI model** structures feedback by identifying:
1. **Situation** - The specific moment or setting
2. **Behavior** - The observable action taken
3. **Impact** - The measurable effect on people or outcomes

## Key Insight

As Kim Scott emphasizes: "Care Personally, Challenge Directly." This means honest conversations aren't cold - they stem from genuine investment in someone's growth.

## Before Engaging in Difficult Discussions

- [ ] Verify your motivation (growth, not frustration)
- [ ] Maintain two-way dialogue
- [ ] Commit to actionable next steps
- [ ] Focus on behavior, not personality
