# Power & Influence Framework

## Skill Overview
This framework helps navigate organizational dynamics and build influence across teams using Jeffrey Pfeffer's power principles. It activates when seeking stakeholder buy-in, managing politics, or leading without direct authority.

## Core Power Sources
Seven leverage points:
1. Expertise
2. Resource control
3. Relationships
4. Information access
5. Formal position
6. Personal charisma
7. Persistent determination

## Strategic Approach

Success requires three steps:
1. Understanding who holds decision-making authority and controls resources
2. Developing genuine relationships while supporting others' success
3. Establishing credibility through incremental wins that build reputation

## Practical Tools

The stakeholder mapping template guides you to:
- Categorize players by influence level
- Identify their priorities
- Assess current alignment
- Design customized engagement strategies for different groups

## Key Insight

As Pfeffer notes: "Power is the ability to get things done." The most impactful professionals cultivate political capital proactively, before requiring it for major initiatives.

## Execution Checklist

- [ ] Map power structures
- [ ] Build coalitions
- [ ] Have individual conversations to surface concerns
- [ ] Tailor pitches to specific motivations
- [ ] Combine rational and emotional appeals
- [ ] Build momentum through early victories
