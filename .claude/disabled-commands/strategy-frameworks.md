# Product Strategy Craft

This skill guides strategic product development using core frameworks.

## Core Frameworks

**Playing to Win** addresses five critical choices:
1. Your winning aspiration
2. Where to compete
3. How you'll gain advantage
4. Required capabilities
5. Necessary management systems

**Crossing the Chasm** emphasizes beachhead strategy - dominating one segment first before expanding to adjacent markets.

## Key Strategic Questions

The frameworks help you answer:
- What does success look like in 3-5 years?
- Which market segment offers the best initial foothold?
- What competitive advantage justifies customer choice?
- What organizational capabilities must you build?

## Practical Application

This skill activates when:
- Defining target markets
- Establishing competitive positioning
- Choosing beachhead segments
- Ensuring tactics align with broader strategy

## Key Insight

Geoffrey Moore emphasizes that "startups often fail by expanding too quickly; focus on dominating one beachhead first."

The Playing to Win framework reinforces that strategy fundamentally requires difficult choices about what to pursue and, equally important, what to deliberately avoid.
