# Executive Communication

This skill helps draft executive memos and stakeholder communications using proven frameworks from leading tech companies.

## Key Frameworks

**Amazon's 6-Pager** structures documents with:
1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
2. Context
3. Analysis
4. Recommendation
5. Next Steps
6. Appendix

**SCQA Framework** guides the narrative arc: Situation to Complication to Question to Answer.

## Essential Elements

Every executive communication needs:
- **BLUF first** - Decision or key message in 2-3 sentences at the top
- **Clear context** - Background readers need
- **Obvious recommendation** - What you're proposing
- **Specific next steps** - Who does what and when
- **Data support** - Facts backing your position

## Writing Principles

Keep documents concise and scannable with headers and bullets.

"If you can't summarize it in 2 sentences, you don't understand it well enough."

Avoid fluff, prioritize action, and present alternatives systematically.

## Use Cases

- Board updates
- Strategic decisions
- Stakeholder announcements
