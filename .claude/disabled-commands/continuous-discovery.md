# Continuous Discovery Habits

## Overview

This framework implements Teresa Torres' approach to integrating customer research into product development through weekly team interactions, visual mapping tools, and systematic assumption validation.

## Core Concept

The foundation rests on a simple principle: "At a minimum, weekly touchpoints with customers by the team building the product, where they conduct small research activities in pursuit of a desired outcome."

## The Three Essential Components

**1. Product Trio Collaboration**
The Product Manager (business viability), Designer (usability/desirability), and Engineer (feasibility) work together throughout discovery. This diverse perspective approach yields superior decisions by combining distinct viewpoints during interviews, analysis, solution generation, and testing.

**2. Opportunity Solution Trees**
This visual framework progresses from business outcomes → customer opportunities → potential solutions → underlying assumptions → validation experiments. It makes thinking transparent and builds stakeholder confidence in decision-making rationale.

**3. Assumption Testing**
Rather than asking customers what to build, the methodology focuses on testing hypotheses about what will succeed. Tests progress from low-risk surveys through interviews, prototypes, manual processes, simulations, and finally live implementations.

## Practical Implementation

Teams maintain weekly discovery cadences including customer interviews, snapshot documentation, tree updates, and assumption testing. Interview snapshots capture participant details, key insights, discovered opportunities, verbatim quotes, and next steps—keeping learning accessible across the team.

The framework prioritizes testing highest-risk assumptions using the lowest-cost methods before committing to development.
