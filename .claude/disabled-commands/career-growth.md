# PM Career Accelerator

## Overview
This skill guides product managers through career advancement using frameworks from 40+ career episodes and Ami Vora's strategies. It activates when planning promotions, identifying skill gaps, navigating transitions, or choosing between IC and management paths.

## Core Frameworks

**Promotion Levels:**
Associate PM to PM to Senior PM to Staff/Principal to Director

The framework evaluates four key dimensions:
- **Scope** (impact size)
- **Complexity** (problem difficulty)
- **Independence** (guidance requirements)
- **Leadership** (influence on others)

**Gap Analysis Approach:**
Systematically compare current state across scope, impact, skills, and leadership dimensions against target level requirements, then develop specific plans to close each gap.

## Action Template: 90-Day Promotion Plan

1. Define target level requirements
2. Map current vs. target capabilities
3. Create monthly action plans addressing scope, skills, and leadership
4. Establish success metrics and evidence

## Key Strategic Insights

Ami Vora emphasizes, "Promotions don't happen to you, they happen because of you."

"You get promoted for doing the next level's job, not for doing your current job well."

## Success Path

Assess gaps - Close gaps through high-visibility projects and skill development - Demonstrate readiness through documented impact and manager alignment.
