# Jobs-to-be-Done Product Design Framework

## Overview
This skill applies Bob Moesta's JTBD theory to product design, shifting focus from features to the actual progress customers seek. As Moesta notes, **"People don't buy products, they hire them to make progress in their lives."**

## Core Concept
Rather than asking "What features do users want?", JTBD asks "What job are they trying to accomplish?" This encompasses three dimensions:
- **Functional**: The practical task
- **Emotional**: Desired feelings
- **Social**: Desired perception by others

## Forces Model
Customer decisions involve four competing forces:

| Force | Direction | Definition |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Push | Away | Dissatisfaction with current situation |
| Pull | Toward | Attraction to the new solution |
| Anxiety | Resistance | Fear and uncertainty about change |
| Habit | Inertia | Comfort with existing approaches |

## Practical Application
Use the provided template to analyze any feature by:
1. Writing a clear job story (When/I want/So I can)
2. Mapping all four forces
3. Designing solutions addressing functional, emotional, and social needs
4. Strategically reducing anxiety and switching costs
